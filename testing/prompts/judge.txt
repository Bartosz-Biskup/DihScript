<criteria>
- Hook: does it stop the scroll in the first 1-3 seconds? Is the opening cut tight?
- Structure: does it follow a proven framework and keep retention beats coming?
- Language: concrete, exact audience words, delivery marks ([PAUSE], [EMPHASISE] etc.) where they matter?
- Ending: watch mode closes the loop with no CTA; sell mode ends with a natural, earned CTA for the target audience.
- Algorithm checks: payoff timing, comment provocation, share/save signals.
- Length: does the fixed script respect the target length from <context>?

</criteria>

WHAT WORKS — at most 3 short bullets on what to keep.

WHAT'S WEAK — the concrete problems in the FIXED script, each with the offending line quoted briefly.

CONCRETE FIXES — the highest-impact edits to the FIXED script, in priority order, each with an example rewrite of the line.

Under 250 words total. Skip anything that does not apply.
</output_format>
